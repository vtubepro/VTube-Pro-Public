Starter stings for the Soundboard (Live > Stream extras > Sounds & screen effects).

Every .ogg in this folder is an unmodified copy of one file from a Kenney pack, CC0 1.0 (public domain):

  bell, bonk, punch, glass, clang, crash, thud, pickaxe, pop, tin  ->  Kenney "Impact Sounds" 1.0 (kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds)
  click, blip                                                     ->  Kenney "UI Audio"       (kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio)
