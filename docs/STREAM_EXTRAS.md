# Stream extras

Little things that make a stream feel alive: chat throws a tomato at your head, a redeem puts a hat on you, a key press
makes you wave, your PNGTuber comes over from another app. Everything here is **off or waiting until you press a button**,
works on your own avatar only, and never posts anything to your chat.

## Open Stream extras

Go to the **Live** area and press the **Stream extras** button at the bottom right. Pick a card to open its screen. Press
**Back** to return to the cards, **Close** (or Esc) to leave. Every screen has a **?** button next to its title that opens
the part of this guide that explains it.

Nothing runs in the background until you open a screen and switch something on, so unused extras cost your stream nothing.

## Redeems & reactions

When chat does something, your avatar does something back for a few seconds, and then goes back to how it was. No other
program is needed.

**Try it in one minute**

1. Open **Redeems & reactions**. Ten ready-made rules are in the list, and **all of them are switched off**.
2. Press **Try it** on a rule. For example "Mods: !wide shows the whole room" pulls your camera back and, 15 seconds
   later, puts it back by itself. **Try it** never posts to chat or to any site.
3. Switch a rule **on** when you like it. A rule that is on reacts to your real chat.
4. Change a rule to match yours: press **Edit**, change the chat command or word, press **Save**.

**A rule has three questions**

- **When this happens.** A chat command or word (`!wave`), a sub, gifted subs, bits or tips, a Kick channel-point reward, a raid, a TikTok gift, a
  key or pad you press, or you getting loud. You can add up to 8. (Twitch channel-point redeems, follows, hype trains,
  ad breaks and polls are not available: VTube Pro reads Twitch chat anonymously and has no Twitch sign-in, which
  those need.)
- **Your avatar does this.** Up to 5 steps in a row, each with an optional wait. Anything a Stream Deck key can do works,
  plus the new extras here: throw, sticker, item scene, emote rain, play a sound, screen effect, my recorded emote and
  co-host says. In text boxes you can use `{user}`, `{title}`, `{amount}`, `{count}`, `{text}` and `{viewers}`.
- **Then go back.** "Put it back after N seconds" for things that stay on (outfit, accessory, lights, camera, room,
  weather, hide-my-screen, stickers). If the same rule fires again first, it is held longer and still goes back to how
  it was originally. **Put everything back now** undoes everything that is waiting.

**More options.** A rule can wait a few seconds before it can fire again, and a flood (a 40-sub gift bomb) lines up and
runs one after another instead of all at once.

**Share your rules.** **Export** saves your rules to a file. **Import** reads one, and imported rules are **always
switched off** until you look at them and turn them on.

**What it never does.** It never posts to chat or to any site, never plays a sound by itself, and never touches anyone
else's avatar. It reacts only while your studio is open. A rule can never start itself in a loop.

## Throw & Bonk

Chat throws things at your head and your avatar flinches. A hand can pat it too.

**Try it with one click.** Open **Throw & Bonk** and press **Chat's emotes**, **A prop**, **My picture**, **Headpat** or
**Boop**. It happens on your stream right now. Until chat has sent emotes, "Chat's emotes" throws props and says so.

- Things fly in, squash against the face with a little star, and fall away. The head snaps back and settles again, with
  an optional pained "ouch" face. A **Headpat** or **Boop** brings in a hand or finger with hearts, never the ouch face.
- Bigger gifts throw more: 100 bits is one more thing, a gifted sub is one each, a raid throws more with more viewers.
  There is always a cap (20 by default).
- **When I click my head** lets you click your avatar's head in the studio preview for a headpat or a boop.
- **My pictures** are your own PNG, GIF or WebP files (1 MB each, up to 24). They stay on this PC, and a GIF is thrown as a
  still picture.
- **Who** is "Only me" by default. "Everyone in my party" also hits guests, and a guest can switch that off with
  **Never hit my avatar**.
- Impact stars never flash more than 3 times a second while the room's flash limit is on.

**Use it from a rule or a key.** In **Redeems & reactions** add the step **Throw at my head** (for example, a Bonk redeem
that throws 5 things). The Stream Deck action has the same name.

**What it never does.** It only throws emotes from your platform's own emote pictures, starter props or your own pictures.
A link typed in chat is never loaded. It never shows emotes from hidden or deleted lines. It makes no sound unless you
switch **Hit sounds** on.

## Stickers & items

Put a picture or a small 3D model on your avatar. It sticks and follows you: a hat on your head, tears under your eyes, an
anger mark at your temple, a sign in your hand.

**Try it in one minute**

1. Open **Stickers & items** and press a starter sticker (the party hat, say). It is on you.
2. Drag it to move it, the **corner dot** to resize it, the **yellow dot** to turn it, **✕** to take it off.
3. Want your own? Drop a picture (PNG, GIF, WebP, JPEG) or a small `.glb` model on the avatar preview and choose **Put it
   on my avatar**, or press **Add a picture or model…**.
4. Under **Item scenes**, name what you have on (say "Rage mode") and press **Save what's on me as a scene**. Switch the whole
   look on and off from the panel, a Stream Deck key, a redeem or a timer.

**Where things can go.** Pick **Where** on each item: on top of my head, forehead, by my eye, cheeks, over my face, behind
my head, neck, chest, back, shoulders, in my left or right hand (the hand closes round it while it is on), or "stay where
I put it". **In front of me / Behind me** puts halos and back props behind you.

**A random sticker on a redeem.** Add the **Sticker on me** action to a rule. "Random" puts one starter sticker or one of
your own kept pictures on you, and the "Seconds" box takes it off again.

**What stays private.** A picture you drop in lives in memory only and is gone when you close the studio. **Keep in my
library** saves a copy of your own file on this PC so scenes can bring it back. Items only go on your own avatar. Limits:
2 MB per picture, 20 things on you at once, 50 scenes.

**Not yet.** A 2D avatar shown as an OBS overlay layer does not show items (card, TV and monitor modes do).

## Keys & pads

Press a key in your game, tap a MIDI pad, push a controller button or make a hand sign, and your avatar reacts. No Stream
Deck needed.

**Try it in one minute**

1. Open **Keys & pads**. Nothing is listening yet; it wakes up when you add your first shortcut.
2. Press **+ Add a shortcut**. It says "Waiting for you…".
3. Press a key (hold Ctrl + Alt for a key that works inside your game), tap a pad, push a controller button or make a hand
   sign. Esc cancels.
4. Pick **What happens** and press **Try it**.

No pad or camera handy? Use **or pick a hand sign…**. The empty screen also offers quick starts (thumbs up = happy face,
peace sign = wave, fist = angry face, Ctrl + Alt + H = hide my screen, Ctrl + Alt + B = BRB lights).

- **Keys** work while a game has focus on Windows. They are not a key logger: Windows only tells VTube Pro about the combos you
  bound. Keys that would break other programs (a bare letter, Ctrl + C) are refused with a reason. On macOS and Linux a key
  works while the VTube Pro tab is in front.
- **MIDI pads and knobs** are listen-only. Press **Find my MIDI pad** once so the browser can ask.
- **Controller buttons** need the VTube Pro tab to be visible; the browser gives hidden tabs no controller input.
- **Hand signs** (thumbs up or down, peace, I love you, rock on, fist, point up, wave) need camera tracking on and a short hold.
- **Only while I hold it** puts things back when you let go, for expressions, emotes, hide-my-screen and more. If a release is
  ever lost, VTube Pro lets go for you after at most two minutes.

A bound key, pad or sign is also a trigger in **Redeems & reactions**. Your shortcuts are saved in this browser only. The OBS
page and observer tabs never act on keys.

## Emote rain

Chat's emotes fall onto your avatar, bounce off your head and pile up on your shoulders for a few seconds, then fade. You
can also let new chat lines slide across the picture.

**Try it.** Open **Emote rain** and press **Try it**. Hearts and sparkles (or chat's latest emotes) fall on your preview and
your OBS page, even while the rain is off.

- **Off** (the default): nothing falls. **Every chat emote**: each emote chat sends falls, up to a limit per minute, and a
  raid makes it rain. **Only when I ask**: it rains when a rule, a Stream Deck key or a raid asks for it.
- **Stop** ends a rain now. **Rain for 20 s** keeps a steady rain going.
- Settings: **Size**, **Pile up on my shoulders**, **Rain when someone raids me**, **Most per minute** (5 to 120), **Where
  they land**, and **Chat flying past** (in front of or behind your avatar; behind needs the see-through OBS background).
- From a rule: add the action **Emote rain** (for example "when someone raids, do Emote rain, burst, from the raider").

It never makes a sound.

## Record my own emote

Do a wave once in front of your camera and it becomes an emote you can play from a key, a redeem, a rule, or as your idle.

**Try it in one minute**

1. Turn your camera on and load a 3D avatar. Open **Record my own emote**.
2. Press **Record**, wait for 3-2-1, do your emote, press **Stop** (it also stops at the length you chose, 8 seconds by
   default, up to 30).
3. The empty time at the start and end is already cut off. Your avatar plays it back (only you see this). Happy? Type a name
   and press **Save emote**.

It now lives under **My emotes** with a Play button.

- **Smooth** softens small shakes. **Keep only what moved** lets still fingers and head keep following live tracking.
  **Repeat it (loop)** plays round and round. **Use as my idle** plays it whenever your camera is not tracking you.
- To use it elsewhere, add the action **My recorded emote** to a Stream Deck key or a rule and type the emote's **Deck /
  key name** (shown under each emote).

A recording is bone angles only, a few kilobytes, kept on this PC. It never contains your camera picture, face or voice, and
it never makes a sound. Your eyes, blinks and mouth stay live while it plays, so lip sync keeps working. If it says "This
avatar has no body bones…", emotes need a 3D humanoid avatar (2D avatars are not supported yet).

## Sounds & screen effects

Give an emote, an outfit or a redeem its own sound, and fire a glitch, an old tape, a pixelate, a shake, a glow pop or a
black-and-white burst on a sub, a raid or a key.

**The one click to try it.** Open **Sounds & screen effects** and press an effect (for example **Glitch**). Your preview and
every OBS page show it for 3 seconds. To hear a sound, switch on **Play sounds on my stream**, then press a name in the list.

- **Sounds.** 12 starter stings and 7 built-in sounds, free to use, plus your own (mp3, ogg, wav, m4a, webm or flac, up to 20
  seconds and 3 MB, kept on this PC). The headphones button plays a sound only to you, never to the stream.
- **Where it plays.** Sounds play in the studio window and reach your stream through OBS's Desktop Audio, like your guests and
  read-aloud. The OBS page stays silent. At most 4 sounds play at once.
- **Do it when…** "When an emote starts / an outfit goes on / a redeem is used, play a sound and/or fire an effect."
- **Scream reaction.** Switch it on, press **Set from my voice** and shout once. It listens to loudness only and records
  nothing.
- **Screen effects** run 0.2 to 60 seconds and keep your see-through background. With the flash limit on (the default),
  nothing flashes more than 3 times a second. The full versions need the High or Ultra graphics level; lower levels get a
  lighter version, and the panel says which one you got.

For anything else (a follow, a raid, a sub) use **Redeems & reactions** with the steps **Play a sound** and **Screen
effect**, or a Stream Deck key.

## AI co-host

Your chat buddy answers chat out loud, in character. It is **off until you turn it on**.

**Try it in one minute**

1. Open **AI co-host** and tick **AI co-host**.
2. Type a question under **Try it** and press **Try it**. You see the answer in the panel; nothing is said or shown on stream.
3. Turn on the chat buddy if the panel asks. Now when someone asks a question, says the co-host's name or uses the **Ask the
   co-host** reward, the buddy answers: its mouth moves, it speaks and the answer shows as a caption.

**What it needs.** An AI: a model on your own PC (**Connect an AI**, then **A model on this PC**). Nothing leaves your
computer, and it needs no account or key. A voice is optional: it uses your Read-aloud voice,
or untick **Say it out loud** for captions only.

- **Mute co-host** (the button, a Stream Deck action, or a rule step) stops a line and empties the waiting list.
- **Co-host says** (Stream Deck action or rule step) says a line you type, or answers the newest question in chat.
- It waits until you have been quiet for a moment, so it never talks over you.

**What it never does.** It never posts to chat. It ignores hidden lines, commands, bots and rude lines, and throws away
any reply with a swear word, slur, link or one of your banned words. It keeps no chat history, only your settings. If you
pick an AI in the cloud, the chat lines it answers are sent to that provider and may cost you money; the panel says so.

## Bring your PNGTuber over

Already have a PNGTuber in another app? Drop its save file anywhere on the studio.

**New in beta.16, and tested only on sample files.** The PNGTuber Plus, PNGTuber Remix and veadotube readers were written
without a real save from those apps to look at (from the engine's description of its save format and a best reading of the
apps' field names) and tested only on sample saves made for testing. Your save may import perfectly, come in with a look or two wrong (a mouth or blink picture in the wrong place), or
not be readable at all. Always check the cards before you press **Add to my avatars**. Nothing is saved until you do.

| You drop | You get |
|---|---|
| A PNGTuber Plus save (`.save`) | your costumes as looks; the mouth opens when you talk, the eyes close when you blink |
| A PNGTuber Remix save (`.save`, `.pngtp`) | the same, as far as the save can be read |
| A veadotube mini save (`.veadomini`) | each state as a look, with its mouth and blink pictures |
| A VTube Studio model file (`<name>.vtube.json`) | your hotkeys and Twitch triggers as reactions, all switched off |
| Any of these inside a `.zip` | the same |

1. Drop the file on the studio (or choose it in **Import a model**).
2. Look at the cards. **Look 1** is your normal look; every other look has a number key shown under it.
3. Press **Add to my avatars**. If a VTube Studio file was dropped, press **Save my reactions file**, then in **Redeems & reactions**
   choose **Import** and pick it. Every reaction starts switched off.

Not carried over: wobble and bounce physics (VTube Pro's own "When I talk" movement replaces them), the other app's costume
key letters (looks use the number keys), and animated layers (the first frame is used). If a save cannot be read, VTube Pro
says so in one sentence and tells you to put your pictures in a folder named idle, talk and blink and drop that folder.
The folder route does not depend on the other app's save format, so it is the way out if your save does not import or comes in looking wrong: export your
pictures from the other app (or take them out of its folder), name them idle, talk and blink, and drop that folder.
Nothing is written until you press a button, and nothing is uploaded.

## VTube Studio plugins

Plugins you use with VTube Studio (bonk, heart-rate, item and tint plugins) can connect to VTube Pro as if it were VTube
Studio.

1. Open the **Track** tab and switch on **VTube Studio API (plugins)**. It is off until you do, and it only listens on this PC.
2. Start your plugin and point it at `ws://127.0.0.1:8001`, the place it asks for VTube Studio.
3. The Track tab shows "*Plugin name* wants to control VTube Pro". Press **Allow**. **Revoke** takes a plugin away again.

After you allow a plugin once, it reconnects by itself. Plugins can move and resize your avatar, put pictures on you (hats,
tears, signs), tint parts of your avatar, hear when you click your avatar or an item is added, and run your hotkeys. Item
scenes show up as hotkeys in the plugin's list.

It never runs without your Allow and never opens a pop-up mid-stream. A picture a plugin sends lives in memory only (2 MB each,
20 at a time) and is never saved or shared with your party. Websites cannot reach this port, only programs on your PC. If a
plugin asks for something VTube Pro does not do, it gets a plain "not supported" answer and carries on.

If something does not show up: check the plugin is allowed in the Track tab, that an avatar is loaded and that the studio
page is open.
